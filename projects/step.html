STEP — School Transparency and Engagement Portal

A web-based student-government portal designed to make project information, financial records, and organizational activity easier to access, review, and verify.

Project contextUndergraduate team capstone
ClientKLD Central Student Government
TeamFour BSIS students
My contributionRequirements, coordination, research, evaluation, presentation

01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bringing scattered records into one reviewable system.

STEP was developed as a capstone project for the Central Student Government of Kolehiyo ng Lungsod ng Dasmariñas. The existing process relied on different channels for proposals, financial records, meeting information, announcements, and student feedback. This made it harder for students and council members to locate consistent information and review the history behind decisions.

The project proposed a centralized web portal where records could be submitted, reviewed, approved, and made visible according to each user's role. Its financial-record integrity design uses SHA-256 hash chaining so later changes to linked approved records can be detected. This makes the records tamper-evident; it does not make the system a decentralized blockchain or prove that every original entry is correct.

02 · THE PROBLEM

Information was fragmented across tools and processes.

Unclear financial historyTransactions, amounts, and supporting evidence were stored in separate files and channels.
Fragmented recordsProject decisions, meeting information, and announcements were distributed across different platforms.
Inconsistent feedbackOccasional polls and forms made student feedback harder to collect consistently.

03 · THE SOLUTION

A centralized portal with role-based review.

The system brings project proposals, a financial ledger with evidence, approval and rejection workflows, meeting records, ratings, concerns, and audit/reporting features into one application. Role-based access control separates the work of preparing records, reviewing submissions, administering the platform, and viewing approved information.

Project & finance recordsSubmit project proposals, ledger entries, and supporting evidence.
Review workflowsRoute submissions for approval or rejection with reasons and role-specific access.
Student engagementProvide access to approved information and ways to submit ratings and concerns.
01PrepareOfficers create records and attach evidence.
02ReviewAuthorized administrators inspect submissions.
03Approve or returnRecords are approved or rejected with a reason.
04Publish & verifyApproved records become visible by role; linked changes can be detected.

04 · USERS & ACCESS

Permissions reflect organizational responsibilities.

MemberStudents and teachers who view approved projects, financial records, evidence, and meetings, and can submit ratings or concerns.
CouncilCSG officers who create and manage project proposals, ledger entries, supporting proof, and meeting records, then submit items for review.
AdminThe CSG Adviser and SADU Head, who review submissions, approve or reject with a reason, and oversee relevant records and logs.
Super AdminSystem-level account and permission management, audit/reporting functions, and administrative oversight.
