01 / SYSTEMS ANALYSIS & DEVELOPMENT

TEAM CAPSTONE · KLD CENTRAL STUDENT GOVERNMENT

STEP — School Transparency and Engagement Portal

STEP is a web application designed to centralize student-government project information, financial records and supporting evidence, meeting records, and student feedback. It combines role-based access and review workflows with a SHA-256 hash chain intended to make later changes to linked approved records detectable.

The challenge

Records and communication were spread across spreadsheets, messages, social posts, paper evidence, and shared storage, making information harder to review consistently.

The project response

A centralized portal with project and finance workflows, supporting evidence, approvals, meeting information, and student feedback.
